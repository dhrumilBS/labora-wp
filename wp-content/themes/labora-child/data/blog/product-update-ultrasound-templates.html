Reporting doctors spend much of their day writing the same report structures again and again. This update makes that faster: a template library for ultrasound, X-ray, CT, and MRI reports, and a quicker way to review and sign.

A template library for every modality

Each study type now has its own report template, with the sections your radiologists already use: technique, findings by organ or region, and impression. Doctors open the template for the study, complete the findings, and keep their own wording where they need it.

  • Ultrasound templates for common studies, from abdomen and pelvis to thyroid, obstetric, and Doppler studies.
  • X-ray, CT, and MRI templates organized by body region.
  • Your own templates, copied from an existing one and adjusted to your center's style.
Choose the study, complete the findings, and sign. The template keeps the structure consistent across doctors. Recreate your five most common studies as templates first. They cover most of a typical day's reporting.

Faster review and signing

Reports waiting for a signature now appear in one list, oldest and urgent first. From that list, a doctor can open a report, review it next to the patient's lab results on the same case, and sign it in one step. Signed reports are delivered to the patient and the referring doctor at once.

  1. Open the waiting list to see every report ready for review.
  2. Review in context, with lab results for the same patient alongside.
  3. Sign and send in one click.

Getting started

The templates and the review list are available to every center using radiology reporting. Your account team can help recreate your existing report formats as templates.
