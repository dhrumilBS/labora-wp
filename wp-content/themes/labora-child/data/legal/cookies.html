What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a similar way. They can be needed for a site to work, or used for analytics and advertising.

What you are asked depends on where you are: in some places, such as the European Union and the United Kingdom, non-essential cookies run only after you allow them; in the United States, you can opt out at any time. Do Not Sell or Share

Some advertising and analytics cookies may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information under US state privacy laws. If you are in the United States, use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link at the bottom of every page to opt out on this browser.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

If this changes

If we add cookies in the future, we will list them here first, and non-essential ones will follow the choices you made.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of a website from working.
